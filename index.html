How this is calculated

This is an independent estimate, not information from TaxDome. Your actual quote can differ, for example because of negotiated discounts, credits for payments run through TaxDome, rollover of unused accounts, or contract terms.

    Where sources disagree

    • Price per extra account: trade press says $12 down to $6 depending on pack size; a user-posted price card shows $12, $9 and $7.50 (prepaid) and $13.50, $10.50, $9 (mid-term). We show a range between them.
    • What counts as active: help-center and pricing page reportedly contradicted each other on logins; users say a login counts, a price tracker says it no longer does. Check your own account.
    • Old price: $1,000 per seat is the list price; users report paying $600 to $800 per seat or multi-year plans.
    • The 50% discount on extra accounts is a statement quoted in trade press, not on the pricing page. The terms are unknown.
