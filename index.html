What will TaxDome cost your practice after the switch to per-client pricing?

Since Nov. 2, 2026 TaxDome charges per active client instead of per seat. Current customers keep their plan and price until their first renewal on or after Jan. 1, 2027. TaxDome's own calculator gives you the new list price. This page gives you the same price (checked against it) and adds what it does not: the change against what you pay now, the price per client, what happens if you add clients mid-year, and what other tools cost for the same number of users.

How this is calculated

This is an independent calculation, not information from TaxDome. Your actual quote can differ. The TaxDome list price is derived from TaxDome's pricing page (taxdome.com/pricing, read Nov. 24, 2026) and checked against 17 values of TaxDome's own calculator.

Price per extra client

The first 150 active clients cost $1,500 per year, even with fewer than 150. Each active client above 150 is an extra client, priced in volume bands. TaxDome applies the lowest total for your size: the price is the lower of (extras x the rate of your band) and (the start of the next band x its rate), rounded up to whole dollars.

    What the pricing page says

    • Logins do not count. Logging in or browsing the app does not make a client active. Qualifying activity does, and a client stays active until your plan year ends.
    • Rollover: active clients you paid for but did not use roll into your next plan year in full, as bonus clients, and are used first. Bonus clients still unused after that year can roll over again, up to 20% of your next year's purchase. Carried-over clients cannot be refunded.
    • Growth during the year: at the end of each month TaxDome counts active clients above your plan and invoices the overage, starting at $15 per client. A volume pack bought at any time lowers the rate. The page does not say how a pack is priced; the estimate above treats it like the calculator's total for the larger size.
    • Multi-year plans: not offered right now. Plans are billed annually.
    • Add-ons: AI $200 per month, Managed Client Care $100 per month, Premium support $249 per month. White-labeled client app and Extended API: price on request. Only AI can be ticked above.
    • Above 3,000 active clients or 20 seats: custom pricing.

    What the pricing page does not say

    • The 50% discount on extra clients for smaller existing customers. It comes from a statement by TaxDome's president quoted in trade press. Terms unknown. That is why it is shown as a separate scenario and never replaces the list price.
    • Whether you can renew at the old price before Jan. 1. The page only says current customers keep plan and price until their first renewal on or after Jan. 1. Ask your account team and get the answer in writing.
    • A prepay discount. Not on the page.

    Limits of this calculation

    • Checked at 17 points of TaxDome's calculator, all exact. The band starts at 50 and 200 extra clients are only bracketed by those points, and the bands between 2,350 and 2,850 extra clients were not checked one by one. Values in between follow the lowest-total rule above.
    • Not modeled: rollover of unused clients, the Client Care, Premium support and other add-ons, taxes.
    • Old price: $1,000 per seat is the list price from trade press; users report paying $600 to $800 per seat or multi-year plans. Enter your own figure above.
    • Alternatives: monthly list price x 12 x users, annual billing, from vendor pages and user posts researched on 2026-11-24, partly second-hand. Per-return, identity-check and card fees are listed as extras, not added in. Prices change; check the vendor before deciding.
